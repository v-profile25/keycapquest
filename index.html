Getting Started

How to Type

1. Find home row

Rest your left-hand fingers on A S D F and your right-hand fingers on J K L ; — that's called home row, because every other key is just a short reach away from it. Feel for the little bumps on the F and J keys with your index fingers. That's how you find home row without looking down.

2. One finger, one color

Every key on the keyboard belongs to one finger, and that finger never changes. The color shows you which one. Compare your hands below to the keyboard underneath — same colors, same fingers.

3. A few tips

  • Keep your eyes on the screen, not on your hands.
  • Return to home row after every key you press.
  • Use the same finger for the same key every time — it becomes automatic.
  • Go slow at first. Speed comes after accuracy, not before.
  • While you play, watching hands will light up to show exactly which finger to use next.
